**Top 50, ranked by observed demand.**

## Methodology, and what these numbers are not

> **There are no search volumes in this document, because none were
> obtainable.** No Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or Search Console access
> existed in this environment. Inventing numbers would have made the document
> look more authoritative and be worth less.

What *was* obtained is real and geo-targeted: **Google Autocomplete, queried
with `gl=bd`** (Bangladesh) and `hl=bn` / `hl=en`, across 44 seed terms, on
18 November 2026. 388 unique suggestions were harvested.

Autocomplete is a genuine demand signal — Google orders suggestions by query
popularity among real users in the specified region. Two measures are used:

| Signal | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **AC rank** | Position in the suggestion list (1 = most popular completion of that seed). Lower is more searched. |
| **Seeds** | How many different seed terms surfaced this query. Appearing under several unrelated seeds is the strongest available evidence of broad demand. |

**AC rank is relative popularity within a seed, not absolute volume.** A rank-1
suggestion for a rare seed can be searched less than a rank-5 suggestion for a
common one. Treat the tiers below as ordered bands, not as a ranked list of
absolute volume. Verify with Keyword Planner before committing budget.

`Fit` = how well the query matches a shop selling **printed** Bangla-medium
JLPT books: ★★★ direct, ★★ adjacent, ★ parent-brand or poor fit.

## Four findings that change the strategy

### 1. "PDF" is everywhere, and it is the biggest commercial threat

`জাপানি ভাষা শিক্ষা বই pdf` (AC 1) · `জাপানি ভাষা শিক্ষা বই pdf download` (AC 2) ·
`jlpt n5 books pdf` (AC 2) · `japanese language book pdf` (AC 2) ·
`japanese book bangla pdf` (AC 2) · `minna no nihongo n5 bangla pdf` (AC 2) ·
`জাপানি ভাষার বই pdf` (AC 2) · `জাপানি ভাষা শিক্ষা বই n5 pdf` (AC 3)

A large share of this market is **looking for the book free, not looking to buy
it.** This was not visible in the earlier research and it is the single most
important thing the keyword data says.

**Do not target the pdf queries with product pages** — the intent is wrong and
the traffic will not convert. But do not ignore them either. The right response
is the sample reader the shop already has: a page that offers a *genuine free
sample chapter* answers the "I want to read it without paying" intent honestly,
captures the searcher, and converts a fraction. See `content-roadmap.md` and the
recommendation in §4 below.

### 2. The migration motive is confirmed, not hypothesised

`জাপানে যাওয়ার সহজ উপায়` (AC 1) · `জাপানে চাকরির সুযোগ` (AC 1) ·
`জাপানি ভাষা শিখে জাপান যাওয়ার উপায়` (AC 1) ·
`জাপানি ভাষা শিখে জাপান যেতে কত টাকা লাগে` (AC 2) · `জাপানে চাকরির বেতন` (AC 3)

`business-analysis.md` listed "study/work in Japan" as a **hypothesis**. The
autocomplete data confirms it as a dominant motive. This is why the career
pillar in `content-roadmap.md` (item 13) should be promoted, and why level
pages should mention what each level unlocks.

### 3. Course demand dwarfs book demand — and it belongs to the parent brand

`জাপানি ভাষা কোর্স` (AC 1) · `japanese language course bangladesh` (AC 1, 2 seeds) ·
`japanese language school bangladesh` (AC 2) · `japanese language institute bangladesh` (AC 3) ·
`জাপানি ভাষা শেখার ট্রেনিং সেন্টার` (AC 5) ·
`জাপানি ভাষা শিক্ষা কোর্সে ভর্তির বিজ্ঞপ্তি 2026` (AC 3)

This is **Nihonova Academy's** territory, not the shop's — and the `Organization`
/ `parentOrganization` markup added in this pass is exactly the right
substrate. Course searchers are book buyers. The two domains should link to
each other deliberately, and the shop should capture book intent from academy
traffic.

### 4. Price queries are high-intent and currently unowned by any strong page

`japanese book price in bangladesh` (AC 1) · `japanese n5 book price in bangladesh` (AC 2) ·
`jlpt n5 book price` (AC 3) · `japanese language book price in bangladesh` (AC 3)

A SERP check on `japanese n5 book price in bangladesh` returned Rokomari
listings at ৳150–৳300 and BooKVandar's Sharif's N5 at ৳480. **Nihonova's
titles did not appear.** These are transactional queries the shop can win with
pages it now has — but it must be prepared to be compared on price, and the
answer to that comparison is "written in Bangla by the academy that teaches it",
stated on the page.

## Deliberately excluded, and why

| Query | AC rank | Why excluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `জাপানি ভাষা শিক্ষা বই pdf` and the whole pdf family | 1–3 | Intent is "free", not "buy". High traffic, near-zero conversion for a print shop. Address with the sample reader, not a product page. |
| `জাপানি ভাষা কোর্স`, `japanese language course bangladesh`, `…school/institute bangladesh` | 1–3 | **Parent brand's territory.** Belongs on `nihonovaacademy.com`; the shop should be linked from it, not compete with it. |
| `jlpt result`, `jlpt login`, `nat test result`, `jlpt n5 result` | 1–3 | Navigational to the official exam body. Unwinnable and worthless. |
| `minna no nihongo`, `minna no nihongo n5 bangla pdf` | 1–2 | Competitor/third-party product. Only addressable through an honest comparison article, never a product page. |
| `কানজি বরন শাড়ি`, `kanjivaram saree` | 1 | **Harvesting artefact** — "kanji" collides with Kanjivaram sarees in Bangladeshi search. Noted so nobody optimises for it by accident. |
| `japanese boy names`, `japanese in bangladesh`, `japan job without japanese language` | 1–3 | Off-intent for a bookshop. |

   Planner once a Google Ads account exists. Autocomplete shows ordering, not
