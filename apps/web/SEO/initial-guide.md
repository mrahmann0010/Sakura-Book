- Organization.
- WebSite.
- BreadcrumbList.

- Offer.

Avoid unsupported properties, fabricated ratings, and misleading markup.

- Bangla.

### Research before implementation

Do not immediately start editing code.

First understand the application, inspect the live website, research the market and develop the SEO strategy.

Then implement the improvements.

### Evidence-based decisions

Never fabricate:

- Competitors.
- Search volumes.
- Keyword difficulty.
- Search rankings.
- Traffic estimates.
- Backlink statistics.
- Customer demographics.

Use actual evidence wherever possible and explicitly label assumptions.

The objective is not simply to increase organic traffic.

The objective is to attract the right audience, increase relevant organic visibility, generate qualified visitors, improve product discovery and ultimately increase book purchases.

Prioritize keywords that have commercial relevance, not merely high search volume.

### Think beyond conventional SEO

Explore opportunities in:

- Google organic search.
- Image search.
- Google Shopping, if eligible.
- Rich results.
- Educational search queries.
- Long-tail queries.
- Brand search.
- Relevant AI-powered search experiences.

Only recommend tactics that make sense for the actual business.

### Be autonomous but careful

You have permission to inspect the repository, conduct research, create documentation, and implement appropriate SEO changes.

Do not ask me to approve every minor decision.

However, do not perform destructive changes, production deployments, DNS modifications, purchases of SEO tools, or changes to live infrastructure without authorization.

### Final expectation

I expect a real SEO improvement project, not a superficial audit.

Think independently, challenge existing assumptions, identify opportunities competitors may be overlooking, and make technically sound improvements.

**The final result should be a better-optimized application with a clear competitive SEO strategy, not just a collection of SEO recommendations.**
