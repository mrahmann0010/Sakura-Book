# Business Context — Nihonova Academy / Nihonova Books

## What this is

Nihonova Academy is a Bangladeshi Japanese-language education brand. Nihonova
Books (this app) is its e-commerce arm: an online bookshop selling original,
in-house-designed books for learning Japanese, written for Bangladeshi
students in Bangla.

This is not a reseller or curator of third-party titles — Nihonova designs
and publishes its own books, built specifically for the way Bangladeshi
students learn: Bangla explanations, exam-focused structure, and material
paced for self-study.

## Vision

Make high-quality Japanese language learning accessible to every Bangladeshi
student — starting from zero, through to exam readiness — using materials
designed for them, in their language, delivered to their door.

## Scope

- **Product**: Physical books, authored and designed by Nihonova.
- **Subjects**: Grammar, Kanji, Vocabulary (reading/listening material may
  extend this over time).
- **Levels**: JLPT N5 → N1, covering the full proficiency range.
- **Exams targeted**: JLPT, JFT-Basic, NAT-Test — the three credentials
  Bangladeshi students commonly need for study, work, or visa purposes in
  Japan.
- **Market**: Bangladesh only, for now. Bangla-first content and UX.
- **Channel**: Mobile-first online store — students browse and order from
  their phones.
- **Payments**: Local mobile financial services — bKash, Rocket, Nagad
  (plus COD/bank transfer as fallback).
- **Delivery**: Nationwide home delivery within Bangladesh.

## Expertise / positioning

- Books are designed, not just compiled — Nihonova treats material design as
  its core competency, not a catalog-curation exercise.
- Bangla-medium instruction removes the English-as-a-bridge-language barrier
  that most Japanese learning material assumes.
- Exam-track alignment (JLPT/JFT/NAT) means content maps directly to what
  students are actually being tested on, not generic language learning.
- Small, deliberate catalog — every title exists because it fills a specific
  gap in a learner's path from N5 to N1, not because of catalog breadth.

## Why this matters for SEO

- Primary audience searches in **Bangla**, not English — "জাপানি ভাষা শেখার
  বই", "JLPT N5 বই", "জাপানি ভাষা কোর্স" style queries are the real demand,
  not English-language Japanese-learning terms which are dominated by
  global, non-Bangladeshi sites.
- Content should be organized around the learner journey (level × exam ×
  subject), since that's how a Bangladeshi student thinks about what to buy
  next — not around generic bookstore categories.
- "Original, Bangla-authored, exam-aligned" is the differentiator against
  any competitor reselling imported or translated Japanese textbooks — this
  claim should be explicit and structured (schema.org `author`/`publisher`
  on Book/Product markup) so it's machine-readable, not just marketing copy.
- Local payment methods (bKash/Rocket/Nagad) and Bangladesh-only delivery are
  trust signals for local search intent — worth surfacing in metadata/FAQ
  content, since "can I pay with bKash" is a real pre-purchase question.
